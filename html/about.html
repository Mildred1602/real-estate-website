🏢 About Us – PrimeEdge Realty Group

At PrimeEdge Realty Group, we believe real estate is more than just buying or selling property — it's about helping people find their place in the world. Since our founding in 2010, our mission has been to redefine modern living through innovation, integrity, and exceptional service. What started as a small real estate agency in Toronto has grown into a trusted brand known for delivering high-value residential, commercial, and investment properties across Ontario and beyond.

Our founders, David Ross and Elena Morgan, built PrimeEdge on a simple yet powerful vision: to make real estate more transparent, client-focused, and future-driven. With backgrounds in property development and urban design, they brought together a team of passionate professionals who share one goal — to make property ownership not only possible, but truly rewarding.

Over the years, PrimeEdge Realty Group has achieved several milestones, from completing landmark developments in Oakville and Mississauga to managing exclusive luxury estates across the Greater Toronto Area. Our portfolio showcases a diverse mix of modern condominiums, family homes, and eco-friendly projects designed to meet the evolving needs of clients. Every project reflects our dedication to quality craftsmanship, sustainability, and long-term value.

As technology reshapes the real estate industry, PrimeEdge continues to lead the way with AI-driven market insights, virtual property tours, and digital-first client solutions. Our success is built on trust — and that trust has earned us repeat clients, industry awards, and partnerships that stand the test of time.

Today, PrimeEdge Realty Group remains committed to building not just properties, but communities — places where people can grow, connect, and thrive. Guided by our core values of honesty, excellence, and innovation, we continue to shape the future of real estate with the same passion and purpose that started our journey.
